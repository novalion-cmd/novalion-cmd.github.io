Privacy policy

A clear account of the information this private recording uploader uses, and the controls available to its operator.

Effective and last updated: 15 November 2026

1. Application and operator

This policy covers Youtube Uploader 21.8, a personal desktop video uploader, and this accompanying information website. The application is operated by the individual responsible for this personal video uploader. Contact the operator at sharklessfish@outlook.com.

This is independent software, not a Google or YouTube service. The website does not offer public account registration, collect Google credentials, or accept video uploads.

2. Information the uploader accesses

  • Google authorization information: OAuth client configuration, access tokens, refresh tokens, granted permissions and token expiry information needed to connect to Google.
  • YouTube channel identity: the authorized channel’s title and channel ID, used to verify the destination account.
  • Recordings and upload metadata: video files from the configured local folder, filenames and paths, sizes, upload titles, descriptions, tags, category and privacy settings.
  • Operational records: upload status, progress, timestamps, YouTube video IDs, retry information, errors and local upload-history records.

The uploader does not ask for or store your Google password. Google handles account sign-in and any two-step verification.

3. Why the data is used

The uploader uses this information to authenticate requests, confirm the connected channel, upload eligible recordings, apply the operator’s metadata and privacy choices, display progress, manage retries and maintain local history. Configured local cleanup can remove successfully uploaded recordings after the selected retention period.

The application requests youtube.upload for uploading videos and youtube.readonly for channel verification. The read scope grants broader read access, but the uploader’s verification feature uses the channel title and ID. Choosing Google permissions does not itself start an upload.

4. Storage and access

OAuth client files, tokens, application settings, upload history and diagnostic logs are stored on the computer running the uploader, in its configured application-data locations. Recordings remain in the selected local recording folder until the operator or configured cleanup removes them. The uploader uses file permissions and atomic token updates where supported; local storage is not represented as universally encrypted.

Authorized application and private control interfaces may display channel identity, filenames, job status and diagnostic information to permitted operators. Those interfaces require their own access configuration. Backups made by the operator may contain copies of local application data.

The optional authorization-transfer workflow creates a password-encrypted transfer file containing the client configuration, token information and verified channel identity. It is created and copied only when the operator uses export/import. This website does not receive or store that file.

5. Disclosure and Google Limited Use

Recordings and upload metadata are sent to Google/YouTube to perform requested uploads. Authorization information is sent to Google’s OAuth services to obtain or refresh access, and channel details are obtained from the YouTube API. Video visibility is determined by the configured YouTube privacy setting; public videos can be accessible to the public.

The uploader does not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or use it to train AI models. It does not send Google user data to an unrelated analytics service. Access by the operator is for the personal video-upload workflow, maintenance, troubleshooting or security. Any separate sharing initiated by the operator, including support exports and backups, must be handled with appropriate care.

The application’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Google and YouTube process information under their own policies, including Google’s Privacy Policy.

6. Retention and deletion

Authorization files are retained locally until replaced or removed by the operator. Upload history and local recordings have separately configurable retention settings. Automatic recording deletion, if enabled, applies to successfully uploaded files after the configured period; keep independent backups of recordings you want to retain.

To remove local application data, stop uploading, close the uploader and use the installation’s documented data-removal or uninstall options, or remove the relevant files from the configured data folders. Review any retained backups and exported transfer files separately. Removing a local token or recording does not delete a video already uploaded to YouTube. Manage uploaded videos through YouTube Studio.

7. Revoke access or request help

You can revoke this application’s Google access through Google Account — third-party connections. Revocation prevents future authorized API use once Google applies the revocation; it does not automatically erase local files or existing YouTube videos.

For questions or assistance with data associated with this private application, contact sharklessfish@outlook.com. Do not email passwords, OAuth tokens, client-secret JSON files, authorization callback URLs or private recordings. The operator may need non-secret information to identify the installation or issue.

8. This website and hosting

This is a static information site. Its pages do not add analytics, advertising, account registration, contact forms or tracking cookies, and they do not access the uploader’s local files. Contact links open your email application; any email you choose to send is handled by your email provider and the operator.

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical request information, such as IP addresses, browser information and access/security logs, under GitHub’s Privacy Statement. Website hosting is separate from Google authorization and video storage.

9. Changes to this policy

Updates will be published on this page with a revised effective date. Material changes to how the uploader accesses, uses or shares Google data should be disclosed before the changed use begins, with additional consent obtained where required.
