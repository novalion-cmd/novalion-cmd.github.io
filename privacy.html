Privacy policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

This policy describes how Youtube Uploader 21.8 handles information when you connect a Google account and upload videos. For questions or requests, contact the application operator at sharklessfish@outlook.com.

Information used by the application

  • Videos you select and their upload details, including titles, descriptions, tags, category and privacy settings.
  • The authorized YouTube channel's name and ID.
  • OAuth client configuration, access and refresh tokens, granted permissions and expiry information.
  • Upload records, including filenames, progress, timestamps, video IDs, errors and retry status.

The application does not collect your Google password. Sign-in and permission approval are handled by Google.

How information is used

The information is used to connect your account, confirm the destination channel, upload videos with your chosen settings and display upload progress and history. The application requests the youtube.upload permission for uploads and youtube.readonly to retrieve the channel name and ID. The read permission grants broader read access, but the application's channel check uses those identity details.

Storage and sharing

Settings, authorization files and upload records are stored in the application's configured data folders on the computer running it. Video files stay in their selected folder until you or a configured cleanup option removes them. Authorized application controls may display account and upload status. Backups can contain copies of this information.

Videos, upload details and authorization requests are sent to Google/YouTube as needed to perform these functions. Video visibility follows the privacy setting chosen for the upload. Google handles this information under Google's Privacy Policy.

Google user data is not sold, used for advertising or used to train AI models. The application does not send that data to unrelated analytics services. Access by the operator is limited to providing the application, maintenance, support and security purposes.

The application's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Security and retention

Authorization files are protected using the operating system's file permissions where supported. They remain sensitive files and should not be shared. Optional authorization exports are password-encrypted; protect their passwords and any backup copies.

Authorization files remain until replaced or removed. Upload history and video retention depend on the application's settings. If automatic cleanup is enabled, successfully uploaded local video files can be deleted after the selected retention period. Keep backups of videos you wish to retain.

Removing access or data

You can revoke access at Google Account — third-party connections. To remove local data, stop uploading and remove the relevant files from the application's data folders or use its data-removal options. Review backups and exports separately. Revoking access or deleting local files does not delete videos already uploaded to YouTube; manage those through YouTube Studio.

This website

This is a static information website. It does not add analytics, advertising, tracking cookies, account registration or contact forms. It does not receive your videos or authorization files. Email links open your own email application.

The site is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical request information, such as IP addresses and access logs, under GitHub's Privacy Statement.

Changes and contact

Updates will appear on this page with a revised date. Material changes to the use of Google data should be disclosed before they take effect, with additional consent where required. For help, contact sharklessfish@outlook.com. Do not send passwords, tokens, client-secret files or private videos.
