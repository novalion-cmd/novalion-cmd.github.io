2. Authorized use

Use the application only with Google accounts and YouTube channels you own or are authorized to manage. Upload only recordings and other content for which you have the necessary rights and permissions, including permission from people whose data or content appears in a recording where required.

Use must comply with applicable law, YouTube’s rules and the YouTube Terms of Service. Do not use the application to evade platform restrictions or access another person’s account.

3. Account and upload controls

The operator is responsible for selecting the intended Google account and channel, reviewing requested permissions, protecting local credentials and transfer passwords, and checking upload titles, descriptions and privacy settings. Confirm the verified channel and queued files before starting uploads.

Starting the uploader can process eligible recordings in its configured watched folder. Changing this folder changes which files may be uploaded. Review automatic local deletion and retention settings before enabling them, and keep independent backups of important recordings.

4. Availability and third-party services

The application depends on local computers, network connectivity, Google OAuth, the YouTube Data API, quotas and third-party service availability. Uploads can be delayed or fail, and some outcomes may require manual review. Inspect uncertain outcomes before retrying to avoid unintended duplicate uploads.

Access tokens can be refreshed automatically when a valid refresh token is available. Google can revoke or expire authorization or require another sign-in. No promise is made of permanent authorization, uninterrupted uploads, perpetual free hosting or continued availability of any third-party service.

6. Stop use and revoke access

You may stop uploading through the application and revoke its Google access at Google Account — third-party connections.
