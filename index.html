Video uploader for YouTube

Youtube Uploader 21.8 is a small application for uploading videos directly to your YouTube channel. It brings the upload settings and progress information together in one place.

What it does

  • Uploads video files to a YouTube channel you authorize.
  • Uses the titles, descriptions and privacy settings you choose.
  • Shows the connected channel and the status of uploads.
  • Keeps upload history to help you follow completed and unsuccessful uploads.

Connecting your account

You sign in on Google's own website and choose whether to allow the application access. The application does not ask for your Google password. Check the channel and upload settings before starting an upload.

The application requests youtube.upload to upload videos and youtube.readonly to check the connected channel's name and ID. You can remove its access from your Google Account.

More information

Read the privacy policy for information about data and permissions, or the terms of use. This website provides information about the application; it does not accept video uploads or Google credentials.
