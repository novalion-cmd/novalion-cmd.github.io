Confirm the connected channel, choose video privacy, stop uploading, and configure local recording retention in the application.

Why Google
authorization is needed.

The application requests two YouTube permissions. They enable uploads and let the operator check the identity of the connected channel before recording archives are sent.

Upload recordings

Sends selected recording files and configured metadata, such as titles, descriptions and privacy settings, to your authorized YouTube channel.

https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload

Verify the channel

Retrieves the authorized channel’s title and channel ID to show which channel is connected. The requested read permission is broader than this identity check; the uploader uses it for verification.

https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.readonly

Authorization stays under your control. Sign-in and consent are handled by Google. OAuth credentials and upload history are stored by the desktop application, not on this website. Access can be revoked through your Google Account.
